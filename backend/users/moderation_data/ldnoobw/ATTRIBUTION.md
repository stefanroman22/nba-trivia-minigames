# LDNOOBW word lists

The files in this folder (`en.txt`, `es.txt`, `fr.txt`, `de.txt`, `it.txt`, `pt.txt`, `ru.txt`, `hu.txt`) are
verbatim copies of the per-language lists in the
**List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words** by Shutterstock and contributors:
https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words

Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Downloaded 2026-10-06 (branch `master`). Changes: none to these files. `../severe.json` and `../mild.json`
are curated selections derived from them (some entries moved to a severe tier, some excluded as benign
everyday words, plus a few inflections); Romanian has no LDNOOBW list, so its few entries are hand-written
and marked `needs-native-review`.

These lists are server-side data only. They are never shipped to the browser.
