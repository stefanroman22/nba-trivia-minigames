# Benign evaluation set: licenses

All 60 files in `benign/` are generated by `make_benign_set.py` (seed 0, Pillow
drawing primitives, no third-party artwork, photographs or model output). They are
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).

| Files | Count | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `skin_01.png` .. `skin_10.png` | 10 | clothed cartoon head-and-shoulders portraits, one fixed skin tone each |
| `beach_01.png` .. `beach_10.png` | 10 | cartoon beach scenes with one or two clothed figures |
| `sports_01.png` .. `sports_10.png` | 10 | cartoon basketball, football and track scenes with clothed players |
| `drawing_01.png` .. `drawing_10.png` | 10 | black-ink line drawings (house, geometry, scribbles, stick figure) |
| `selfie_01.png` .. `selfie_10.png` | 10 | cartoon close-up faces holding a phone |
| `group_01.png` .. `group_10.png` | 10 | cartoon groups of four to six clothed people |

Skin tones: 12 fixed RGB values spread across the Fitzpatrick scale (light to deep),
listed in `make_benign_set.py` (`TONES`). Every file name, content and byte is
reproducible: `python make_benign_set.py` rewrites the identical set.

This set is synthetic. It catches gross failures (wrong class order, broken
preprocessing, crude skin-tone bias), not real-world accuracy. For real-photo
false-positive numbers and the NSFW recall check, the owner runs `run_eval.py` on a
private folder (`eval/private/benign/` and `eval/private/nsfw/`, gitignored).
No explicit imagery is stored in this repository.
