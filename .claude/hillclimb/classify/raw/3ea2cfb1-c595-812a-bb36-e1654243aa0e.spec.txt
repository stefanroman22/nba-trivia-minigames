Current public/robots.txt is minimal: User-agent star, Allow /, Disallow /admin, no Sitemap line. Research findings: Google/RFC 9309 recognize only User-agent, Allow, Disallow, Sitemap; groups do not inherit from the star group, so any dedicated bot group must repeat Disallow /admin itself; Sitemap must be an absolute URL and can be listed even before the sitemap.xml task ships (use the real production domain); Crawl-delay is ignored by Google (recommend omitting); never disallow /_next or any JS/CSS path since this is a client-rendered app and blocking those breaks indexing of rendered content; Disallow is not the same as noindex, a disallowed page can still appear index-only, so keep the existing page-level noindex on /admin as-is rather than relying on robots.txt alone. AI crawlers split into three classes: training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, meta-externalagent, Bytespider), search/answer-engine crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot, Bingbot), and user-triggered fetchers. For a free discoverability-driven trivia site with no proprietary content to protect, the recommendation is to stay fully permissive (allow all of the above) rather than block training bots, since blocking has no upside here and could reduce visibility in AI answer engines. Task: keep the current Allow-all-except-admin shape, add a Sitemap line once the production domain is known (coordinate with the sitemap.xml work in the SEO card), and do not add crawl-delay, host or noindex lines.
