The post-game reveal takes too long. In GameResult.tsx the score is already a known number the instant a game ends (games already track points as the player answers) -- nothing is actually being calculated when the Calculating score... loader shows. In MiniGame.tsx around the showResult effect, the flow is: hide the result, await a POST to /trivia/log-session/ to award points, THEN wait an unconditional setTimeout of 1500ms, THEN reveal. That fixed 1.5s pause is pure dead time with no real work behind it. Fix: show the result immediately (or with a short, under 300ms, polish transition) without gating on the point-award request finishing -- let awardPoints keep running in the background and dispatch the awarded points to redux whenever its response lands, independent of when the reveal animation plays. Keep the existing AnimatedNumber count-up and confetti, they are already fine; only remove the extra artificial wait. Verify in a real browser across at least two different games (e.g. wordle and one non-daily game) that the result appears promptly, points still get awarded correctly, and a slow or failed log-session call does not block or break the reveal.
