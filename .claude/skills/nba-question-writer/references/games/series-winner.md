# Guess the Series Winner (`series-winner`, pool `playoff`) — record-based, not written

One round = one completed playoff series; the player clicks the team that won. 20 points.

## Can the writer add questions? No.

The pool is every completed series from the NBA's playoff game log: 912 live (80 seasons, 1946-47 to
2025-26; First Round 380, Conf. Semis 288, Conf. Finals 160, Finals 80, Division Round Robin 4). About 15
a season arrive with the data refresh. Play-in games and unfinished series are dropped. Tell Stefan this at
step 0 and stop.

## Answers

A click on one of the two team names, compared with `winner`; team names are the season's own. No known
way a correct pick is rejected.
