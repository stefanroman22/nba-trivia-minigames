# Writing style

Players read these on a phone, mid-game, often in a second language. Text that sounds like a
machine wrote it breaks the feeling that a fan made this game.

## Sound human

- Write the way a knowledgeable friend would ask it out loud. Read it aloud; if it sounds stiff, rewrite.
- No em dashes, en dashes or double hyphens (`--`) in player-facing text. Use a comma, a period or
  parentheses. Hyphens inside words and number ranges ("2015-2018") are fine.
- No emojis, no exclamation marks in prompts, no hype words ("legendary", "iconic", "epic", "ultimate").
- No filler openers ("Can you name...", "Do you know..."). Start with the ask: "Name a player who...".

## Keep it plain

- One idea per prompt, under about 15 words. Fan Favorites prompts are capped at 200 characters.
- Common words, active voice, roughly a 6th to 8th grade reading level.
- Make the kind of answer clear up front: a player, a team, a season, a coach.
- Spell out jargon or skip it on easy items ("Defensive Player of the Year", not "DPOY", in prompts).
  Short criteria labels in Tic-Tac-Toe may abbreviate because the cell is tiny.

## Be exact

- State the scope inside the prompt when it changes the answer: "NBA" (not ABA), "regular season",
  "since 2010" (say whether the bound is inclusive: "2010 or later").
- Time-sensitive claims carry their date in the item, not in your head. Prefer settled facts
  ("won MVP in 2016") over moving ones ("current Lakers starter").
- Numbers: use digits and commas ("30,000+ points").

## Names

- Show names as `all-players.json` spells them, including accents (Nikola Jokić). Aliases carry the
  plain and common forms (jokic, the joker) so typing without accents still scores.
- Team names: current official name unless the item is about an era ("Seattle SuperSonics" for a
  2007 question). Never mix franchise names inside one item.

## Before and after

- Before: "Can you name one of the legendary #1 overall picks since 2010 -- who absolutely dominated?! 🏀"
- After: "Name a #1 overall draft pick from 2010 or later"
