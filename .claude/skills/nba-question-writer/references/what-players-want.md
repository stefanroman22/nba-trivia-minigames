# What players want

Research summary (2026-10), with sources. Use it to choose topics and set difficulty.

## Difficulty

- Lean easy to medium. Practitioner guides suggest roughly 30-40% easy, 40-50% medium, 20% hard
  (quizado.com/blog/how-to-plan-pub-trivia-night). Too hard and too easy both cut return visits.
- A 70,000-player study found players preferred easier versions; novelty and suspense mattered
  more than difficulty (researchgate.net, "Is Difficulty Overrated?", CHI 2017).
- Keep the floor reachable and reward depth at the top. Immaculate Grid and HoopGrids score rare
  correct answers higher, so a casual fan can finish and an expert can show off
  (newsweek.com/sports-fans-are-flocking-immaculate-grids-perfect-daily-game-1811636).

## What makes a good item

- Most answers should be something a typical fan has heard of
  (playtriviaca.com/blog/2022/7/9/four-simple-tips...).
- Several routes to the answer (team, era, teammate, moment), so knowing any one of them helps
  (slate.com/culture/2021/09/how-to-write-trivia-questions.html).
- Interesting beats obscure. Clues should be why the subject matters, not filler like birth year
  (ocf.berkeley.edu/~quizbowl/qb-writing.html).
- Progressive clues run hard to easy, and the first clue already points to one answer only (same source).
- Accept reasonable variants, such as surname alone for well-known players
  (sporcle.com/blog/2015/01/create-classic-quiz/).

## What NBA fans enjoy

- Remembering role players and one-season stints ("guy-remembering") is the core joy of grid games
  (slate.com/human-interest/2023/10/immaculate-grid-nfl-mlb-sports-trivia-game.html).
- Daily, limited, shareable puzzles create return visits (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle).
- Themes that work: career paths, shared-team links, playoff moments, records (scoped and dated),
  nicknames, draft history and draft-night trades, franchise moves and old names, era snapshots,
  higher-or-lower stat comparisons, famous duos and coaches.
- No good data exists on which era fans prefer. Mix eras on purpose: roughly a third each of
  pre-2000, 2000-2015 and recent, so neither older nor younger fans are shut out.

## What frustrates them

- Wrong or incomplete answer keys (gspn.substack.com/p/three-gspners-vs-unreliable-nba-trivia).
- Stale facts, such as a scoring-leader question written before LeBron passed Kareem.
- Bad grammar and options that do not fit the question (App Store reviews of NBA quiz apps).
