NBA Wordle

Guess the mystery NBA player in five tries. Each guess is a player name, and every letter turns green when it sits in the right spot, yellow when it is in the name but somewhere else, and grey when it is not in the name at all. A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight, so everyone plays the same player and can compare results with friends.

The answers come from current and past NBA rosters, from household names to role players who spent a season or two in the league. Use the hints wisely: a strong first guess with common letters narrows the field quickly, and the keyboard keeps track of every letter you have already tried.

Want more? Try Guess the MVPs, follow a career path or go back to all games.
