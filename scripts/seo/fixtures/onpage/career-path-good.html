How to play NBA Career Path Quiz

Each round shows the career path of one NBA player, revealed team by team. Every stop lists the seasons he spent there, the games he played and his points per game. Read the first team, then name the player. A wrong guess reveals the next team, so the trail gets clearer the longer you look at it.

A game has five rounds. Type a name in the box and pick it from the list; the list only offers real players, so spelling is never the problem. You can skip a round when the trail means nothing to you.

Scoring

Every round is worth up to forty points. The fewer teams you need to see before the right answer, the more you keep, and a skipped round scores zero. The best possible game is two hundred points, which counts towards your rank and the weekly board.

Tips

Look at the years before the teams. A long first stop usually means a star drafted by that club, while many short stops in a row point to a journeyman. Points per game separate a starter from a bench player quickly, so use them to cut the list of candidates before you type.
